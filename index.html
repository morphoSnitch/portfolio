Residential · Kids' Room

Wardrobe & Desk Wall, Built Around a PC Nook

Combined wardrobe, open shelving, and a computer desk in one continuous wall unit
8307 mm overall span

A single wall unit combining a desk with pull-out drawers, an open shelf tower, and a wardrobe with a dedicated PC compartment. The 500 mm cabinet depth was too shallow for a standard horizontal closet rail, so I re-engineered the hanging rail to run perpendicular to the cabinet width instead — a fix that only surfaces once you draw the section. Also handled: multi-zone LED runs across shelves and toe-kicks, glass shelf brackets, and clearance gaps around the PC bay for airflow.

MaterialMDF, plywood, solid pine, 0.6mm veneer
HardwarePush-to-open, soft-close drawer runners
FinishPainted enamel + veneered & lacquered
Detail solvedPerpendicular rail for shallow-depth closet

Residential · Classical Millwork

5.7m Raised-Panel Wardrobe with a Hidden Pull-Out Table

Full-height wardrobe wall in a traditional raised-panel language
5685 mm overall width

A traditional panelled wardrobe run — cornice, fluted pilasters, and applied mouldings — built to read as classical millwork rather than flat-front cabinetry. Behind one door section, a fold-out table slides out on its own carcass. The main compartments use Push-to-Open fronts while the upper mezzanine uses soft-close, so I mapped both systems across twelve equally-sized door modules without breaking the panel rhythm.

MaterialMDF, plywood, veneer-matched solid wood
HardwarePush-to-Open (main) + Silent System (mezzanine)
FinishVeneered, lacquered to sample
Detail solvedConcealed pull-out table within a panel module
Sample 03 — Curved Bathroom
Drawing excerptDimensioned reflected ceiling plan ↗
Residential · Bathroom / Curved Millwork

Radius Wall Panelling Built Around a Backlit Oval Mirror

Full-room wall & ceiling panelling, floating vanity, backlit mirror
Ø420 mm mirror centre-line

One wall of this bathroom curves into a radius return, so the panelling, the ceiling trim, and the backlit oval mirror all had to be drawn as a single continuous shell rather than flat panels butted at a corner. The stone vanity top is explicitly excluded from the millwork scope and only measured after installation — a coordination note I flagged directly on the drawing so the stone fabricator and the joinery crew don't collide on site.

MaterialMDF (moisture-resistant), plywood, 4mm mirror
LightingLED perimeter + mirror backlight, 220–230V feed
FinishVeneered, lacquered to sample
Detail solvedRadius wall geometry + stone/joinery sequencing

Residential · Bathroom Vanity

Double-Basin Vanity with a Woven Veneer Façade

Ten-drawer vanity run under a book-matched stone top with two undermount basins
2369 mm overall length

Ten drawer fronts are laid out in a basket-weave veneer pattern that has to line up perfectly across the whole run — meaning every drawer box width was derived from the façade grid, not the other way round. Each undermount basin is centred exactly on its drawer face, and I cross-referenced the plumbing rough-in (fan pipe and water points) against the drawer carcasses before finalising depths, since basins and drawer boxes were fighting for the same 700 mm zone.

MaterialMDF, moisture-resistant MDF, veneer-matched solid
HardwareSilent-System soft-close drawer runners
FinishVeneered plinth (brass-tone), lacquered carcass
Detail solvedFaçade-driven drawer grid + plumbing clearance check

Residential · Library / Integrated Appliances

Library Wall Built Around Two Integrated Wine Cabinets

Full-height shelving with drawer bases, a TV niche, and two built-in wine cabinets
2 × Meyvel MV95-KBT2, 1610×595×530

A continuous shelving wall that has to leave two exact, appliance-sized voids for a pair of slim wine cabinets while keeping the shelf rhythm visually even across the whole run. I drew both openings straight from the appliance's own service clearances rather than rounding to the nearest shelf module, then carried the same bay width back through the drawer bases below for a consistent façade line.

Residential · Master Bedroom Wall

Veneered Wall Panelling with a Concealed TV Niche

Full wall panelling with shadow-gap reveals and a hidden ventilation outlet
1366 mm TV niche width

A panelled bedroom wall where the TV recess and an existing ventilation outlet both had to disappear into the same veneer grid without breaking the panel joints. The recess is backed in plywood rather than MDF to carry the TV mount load, and every reveal line was set out to land on a real panel seam rather than an arbitrary grid — the kind of decision that only shows up once you draw the elevation full-scale.

MaterialMDF, moisture-resistant MDF, plywood, veneer
FinishVeneered, lacquered; black enamel shadow-gaps
CoordinationExisting ventilation outlet routed through panel
Detail solvedTV-mount plywood backer within a veneer grid
Sample 07 — Walk-in Wardrobe
Drawing excerptDimensioned elevation & sections ↗
Residential · Walk-In Wardrobe

Symmetrical Wardrobe Wall with a Backlit Centre Mirror

Full-height wardrobe either side of an integrated full-length mirror, LED-lit throughout
4045 mm overall width

Two full-height wardrobe carcasses flank a central backlit mirror, and — because the whole wall is symmetrical — the right-hand assembly mirrors the left rather than being drawn twice. I flagged that mirroring explicitly on every section so the workshop doesn't build two left-handed cabinets by mistake. Every visible horizontal and the six pull-out drawers carry LED strip lighting, run from a single external switch.

MaterialMDF, moisture-resistant MDF, plywood, oak
HardwareBlack oxidised hinges, black anodised hanging rail
FinishPainted panels/fronts + veneered carcass, lacquered
Detail solvedMirrored left/right construction called out for the shop
